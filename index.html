Privacy policy

Scottish Personal Licence Practice (uk.golikovichev.personallicencescotland)
Developer: Mikhail Golikov, golikovichev@gmail.com
Last updated: 13 September 2026

The short version

The app does not collect anything about you. There is no account, no sign-up and no server behind it. Your answers, your progress and your settings stay on your phone. Nobody, including the developer, can read them.

What stays on your phone

The app stores, in its own private folder on your device:

  • the language you picked for explanations;
  • the exam date you entered, if you entered one;
  • which questions you have answered and when they are due again;
  • your mock exam results;
  • your settings, including whether reminders are on.

None of this is sent anywhere. There is no analytics library, no advertising library, no crash reporting service and no tracking of any kind in the app.

Your own Google backup

If you have Android backup switched on for your Google account, the system may copy the app data listed above into your own Google Drive, so that your progress survives a new phone. That copy belongs to you and is made by Android, not by this app. The developer has no access to it. You can turn it off in the Android settings for your Google account.

Paying for the full question bank

The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. The app never sees your card, your name or your address. It asks Google one question, whether this Google account owns the product, and gets yes or no. Google processes payment data under its own privacy policy.

Permissions and why they exist

  • Notifications - only if you switch reminders on. Reminders are created and shown on the device; nothing leaves it.
  • Run at startup - Android erases scheduled alarms when the phone is turned off. Without this permission your reminders would quietly stop after the first restart.
  • Internet and network state - these arrive with the Google Play billing library and are used to reach the store when you buy or restore a purchase. The app makes no other network requests.

Children

The app is a revision aid for the written examination taken by people who apply for a personal licence under the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005. A personal licence cannot be held before the age of eighteen (section 72 of the Act), so the app is for adults. It is not directed at children, it is not advertised to them, and it asks nothing about age or about anything else: it has no account, no profile and no form to fill in.

Not affiliated with any licensing authority

This is an independent revision aid. It is not produced, endorsed or approved by any Licensing Board, awarding organisation, police force or government body, and it is neither the qualification nor the licence. Applying for a personal licence is done with your local Licensing Board, not here.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. There is no server copy to delete, because there is no server. If Android backup made a copy for you, it is removed through your Google account, in the same place you would manage any other backup.

If you want your purchase refunded, that goes through Google Play, not through the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new text appears at this address before the change reaches the app.
